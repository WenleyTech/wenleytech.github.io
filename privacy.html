Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Wenley Technologies LLC, doing business as Wenley Health ("Wenley," "we," "us"), handles information on this website, wenleytech.com. Wenley is based in Silver Spring, Maryland.

What this policy covers

This policy covers wenleytech.com only. Our brands (BeautyJar, WomensHealthPros and Backbeam) each have their own privacy policy on their own websites, which explains how that brand handles information.

Information we collect

This website does not have accounts, sign-up forms or a contact form, and it does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics tools.

  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of your visit. This is used to operate, secure and troubleshoot the website.
  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

How we use information

  • To run, maintain and protect this website.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To meet legal obligations, if required.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run this website, such as our hosting provider, and only as needed for that purpose. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public.

Links to other sites

This website links to our brands' websites and to third-party sites such as LinkedIn and Medium. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not apply to them.

Retention

We keep emails only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records. Hosting logs are kept for a limited period according to our hosting provider's practices.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access or delete information you have sent us by email. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy laws. We will respond to requests as those laws require.

Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect this website and the information we receive. No website or email system is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
